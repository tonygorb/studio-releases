Open it the first time

Studio is not code-signed yet, so your computer asks you once.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Studio into Applications. Open it.
  2. When macOS says it could not verify Studio, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway next to Studio.
  1. Run the -setup.exe. No administrator is needed.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.
